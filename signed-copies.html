Shipping estimator

US shipping is USPS Media Mail — priced by weight only, the same to every US address. Pick a book and format above, then estimate here.

Estimated shipping: —

Media Mail rates, USPS Notice 123, effective Oct 4, 2026. The estimate is based on packaged weight (about 1 lb per paperback, 2 lb per hardcover) and rounds any fraction of a pound up. If the actual postage comes in lower, the difference is refunded; if higher, I'll email you before shipping.

How ordering works

  1. 1. Pick your book and format, then click "Order signed copy" — the order form below will already have your book and format filled in. Then add your name, shipping address, and personalization: who the book is for, what occasion it's for (if any), and anything you'd like written inside.
  2. 2. Pay with PayPal — I'll email you a payment link for the exact total (book price + shipping). PayPal checkout also accepts Google Pay and cards. US orders: book price + the shipping estimate. International orders: I'll email you the exact shipping quote first, then you pay.
  3. 3. I order, personalize, sign & send — your copy is ordered fresh, inscribed with your message, signed, and shipped. Please allow extra time — each one is done by hand.
