Chronicles of the Fae: Volume 1

Prequel — in progress

Before Lyra's story began, there were the women who made her. Chronicles of the Fae: Volume 1 is the prequel to the trilogy — the lives of Lyra's mother, her grandmother, and her Aunt Nyx — and the true stories of the gods of old, as they really happened.

Cops and Killers

Psychological thriller — in progress

Tap for the story ▸

An international arms dealer. The Texas Ranger who has spent a career hunting them. Their chase ends above an experimental time portal vortex, in a fall that rips them both out of the future: he lands in 1940s Dallas and reinvents himself as a Texas Ranger, hiding his identity in plain sight; she lands in 1950s Dallas with no memory and a bullet graze across her forehead.

Unsure of who she is, she falls in love with the Ranger, and they marry, expecting “Happily Ever After” — until, in the middle of childbirth, every memory comes flooding back.

She remembers everything. He has no idea what's coming.

I AM the Other Woman

Memoir — in progress

Tap for the story ▸

Her wife wrote The Other Woman — the story of welcoming her spouse home as a woman after 22 years of hetero marriage, told through unconditional love. I AM the Other Woman is Sarah's sequel, told from her side: what it took to build the courage — and reach the desperation — to risk the most important thing in her life by coming out.

About

SarahMae Stephens is an independent author writing fantasy and science fiction. Her Chronicles of the Fae series follows a world of fae, fire, and shadow, and her new Parallax series ventures into science fiction with Perception.

She lives in the Pacific Northwest, where she writes, develops mobile apps, and keeps busy on a small homestead.
